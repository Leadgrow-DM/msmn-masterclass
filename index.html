★★Why now★★

Investors stopped searching. They started asking.

When an investor wants to understand a market, a strategy, or an operator, they no longer open ten browser tabs. They ask a machine, and the machine answers with whoever has published enough substance to be recognized as a source.

That is a hard problem if you have spent twenty years building relationships offline. It is an enormous opportunity if you move now, because almost nobody in capital raising has.

Podcasting is the fastest way to produce that substance. Not because audio is magic, but because a single conversation generates a transcript, a clip library, a newsletter, and a searchable record of your expertise. That is the raw material both humans and machines use to decide who is credible.
